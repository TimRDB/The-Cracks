## Implementation alignment

Generated geometry was inspected and navigation aligned to the actual artwork rather than the prompt's proposed percentages. Patio centres are (23.5,37.4), (48,37.4), (73,37.4); stairs end at y=49; the shared footpath is y=55; the open parking bay is x=36; the foreground lane is y=92. Coordinates are percentages of the uncropped image. Each staircase uses five segments. Overlapping walkable regions allow exact free-form destinations on the footpath, empty bay, between-car gaps and foreground asphalt; sampled line-of-sight routing prevents paths from cutting through occupied bays. The existing directional sprite is reused with slower travel, footfall lift and a slight lean on stairs. Car make/model descriptions are supplied as game text, not dependent on tiny generated badges.

Each car's foreground occlusion layer uses a separately traced 25-plus-point silhouette around its roofline, mirrors, quarter panels, bumper corners and lower body, preventing surrounding road pixels from hiding the player.

Validation: 27 game tests plus a headless Chrome perspective/exit/stairs/car-passages/free-parking/car-mask/return smoke check. ## Perspective and car passages

All rooms now use the same multi-anchor sizing system instead of unrelated CSS widths plus transform scaling. Each painted door has an anchor at its actual threshold depth. This includes a separate deep anchor for the small front door at the end of the living-room hallway, so the character shrinks while walking into that recess. Outdoors the actual sprite width is 10.2% at patio depth (`y=37.4`), closely matching the painted front-door opening, and grows linearly to 22.4% at car depth in the foreground lane (`y=92`). The latter is calculated so the visible character is approximately `1.8 / 1.4` times the painted car height: a 1.8-metre person beside a 1.4-metre car. Endpoints are clamped.

Walk-graph passages run through the painted gaps at `x=24`, `x=50`, and `x=72.7`, in addition to the empty bay at `x=36`. They join the shared footpath to the foreground lane without crossing a vehicle. Separate foreground copies of the three car silhouettes occlude the character while his feet remain behind the cars; the masks disengage in the foreground lane so he correctly appears in front. Automated and Chromium checks cover the scale endpoints and a complete walk between the silver and blue cars.
